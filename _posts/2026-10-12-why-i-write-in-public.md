subtitle: "Writing is thinking made visible — and visible thinking compounds."
category: writings

Most of what I learn evaporates within a week. Writing it down, for strangers, is the only method I've found that makes knowledge stick, because a reader forces me to find the gaps in my own understanding.

<!--more-->

This blog is that practice. Some posts will be finished essays. Most will be notes from the middle of learning something hard.

## The rule

One post a week, no matter what. Quality follows quantity, not the other way round.
